# Calm requests

1. **Find the real task.** What actually needs to happen, the real deadline (and whether it's real or just habit), and
   who it's for. Often only a small part of the request is truly fixed.
2. **Take the pressure words out:** must, should, have to, right now, if you don't…, and orders stacked one after another.
3. **Use one or two of these, not all:**
   - *a real choice*: "Before dinner or after?", "Dishes or bins tonight?" (only choices you'll honour);
   - *a puzzle to solve together*: "How could we get the bag packed without the morning rush?";
   - *curiosity*: "I wonder how fast the Lego could go back in the box";
   - *indirect wording*: "The dentist number is on the fridge whenever it suits", rather than a direct order;
   - *teamwork*: "Let's do the first bit together";
   - *time flexibility*: "Any time before 6 works";
   - *acknowledging their say*: "You know best how you like your room".
4. **Be honest about what isn't optional.** Say so plainly and why ("the medicine can't wait, it only works if taken on
   time"). Reframe *how* it happens, never pretend there's a choice about *whether*.
5. **Offer 2–3 versions**: playful, plain, and very low-key, and let them pick or tweak.
6. **For Jess's own reminders**, if someone wants this style for themselves or a child, suggest the admin adds it to that
   person's instructions, and write any reminder you set (`set_reminder`) in the same low-pressure way.

Examples to adapt: homework ("Which one looks least boring to start with?"), calling the dentist ("Want me to find the
number so it's there whenever you're ready?"), a tidy room ("Five-minute race before the snack?"), medicine ("Water or
juice with it?"), leaving at 6 ("We're heading out at 6; shoes by the door now or in ten minutes?").

This is about less distress, not more compliance: never manipulative, never fake choices. If it isn't enough (a
meltdown, shutdown, or anyone unsafe), say so and suggest support from their doctor, school or a specialist. Not medical
advice.

## Done when
- The real task and any real deadline are named.
- The versions contain no pressure words, and each uses one or two strategies.
- Anything non-negotiable is stated honestly, with why.
- 2–3 versions were offered to choose from.
