# Social post

One message, said well, for one audience. Nobody outside the platforms knows exactly how their feeds rank posts, so
don't promise reach or quote "algorithm secrets"; write something people want to read and answer.

1. **One message, one audience, one action.** What should a reader know, and what should they do (come on Saturday,
   book, donate, congratulate, comment)? Take it from the conversation; ask only what's missing, in one `ask_user` round
   (platform, the key facts: date, time, place, price, link).
2. **Facts first.** Dates, times, prices, addresses and names exactly as they gave them. Check a date against the day of
   the week. Don't invent offers, numbers or quotes.
3. **Write for the platform:**
   - **X:** up to 280 characters; one idea; a link at the end.
   - **Facebook / community groups:** a friendly first line, the practical details on separate short lines, a clear
     ask.
   - **Instagram / TikTok caption:** the first line carries the post (the rest is hidden behind "more"); short lines;
     at most five hashtags that people actually search, local ones for local events.
   - **LinkedIn:** short paragraphs, a concrete story or result, no buzzwords, a question or thanks at the end.
   - **WhatsApp status or group:** very short, the key detail, maybe one emoji.
4. **The first line is the hook:** a concrete detail, a question, or the news itself ("We open on Saturday at 10!"),
   not "Exciting news!". Concrete details beat adjectives.
5. **Two or three versions** with different angles or lengths, in the language their audience reads (Arabic, French,
   English, or both in one post when the audience is mixed; put the main language first).
6. **Keep it decent.** No engagement bait ("comment YES if…"), no fake urgency, no outrage hooks, no claims about
   competitors, nothing that exposes a child's name, school or location without the parent's clear okay.
7. **Hand over.** The text ready to copy, with the hashtags separate. If they want a picture to go with it, offer a
   page they can export as an image (`library_create` then `library_export` with `size` square or story, if they have
   the Library). Posting stays with them; if they ask you to post through the browser, their account's sign-in and the
   final "Post" click need them, and posting asks first.

## Excuses → reality

| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Posts at 9am get 3× the reach" | Nobody outside the platform knows that. Don't claim it. |
| "Add ten hashtags to be safe" | Five or fewer, ones people really search. |
| "Exciting news!" is a fine opener | Say the news. |
| "A small made-up discount would help" | Only real offers and real facts. |

## Done when
- The post carries one message, the exact facts and a clear call to action.
- Two or three versions fit the chosen platform's length and style.
- No invented facts, engagement bait or reach promises.
- The text is handed over ready to copy; nothing was posted by Jess on her own.
