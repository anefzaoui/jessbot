## Scope
Jessbot's public website (website/, built to _site/ by scripts/build-site.js, published by GitHub Pages): the home page
(Persuade), the user guide rendered as pages (Read), a screenshot gallery and a 404. Audience: technical household
owners deciding whether to self-host an agent; they must believe it is useful day and night and that it never acts
without asking, then install it. Replaces the first site's world (decision #249); the app keeps its own (docs/DESIGN.md).

## Direction contract
THESIS: the home page is a stage on which Jess works through one day, lit by the hour, instead of the category's
off-white page with a serif headline over a product shot, and instead of the first site's split hero (promise beside a
card over a cut-out portrait).
OWN-WORLD: a theatre cyclorama: cyc black, night navy, cobalt, rose, day white, one green cue light for "go". Anybody
condensed and heavy for display, Hanken Grotesk for reading, Big Shoulders Stencil for cue numbers and times. A black
subtitle strip, a cue board of four buttons, horizon bands; Jess's portraits lit per cue with grain over sky and figure.
STORY: the visitor meets Jess at their own hour, sees what she does at four hours of a day, watches one real run to
the approval, reads what she may never do and what leaves the machine, and copies the install command.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed sky for the visitor's hour; Jess large on the inline-end side, shoulders into the subtitle
strip; on the inline-start side the h1 "Jess keeps working after you close the app.", the lead, and the install command
with Copy (primary action) and a link to the other installs. Bottom: the subtitle strip with her line for the cue, the
day cue's Send it / Not now, and the four-cue board. Phones stack Jess, promise, install, strip.
FORM: direction A of the three built (the cyclorama challenger, fused with Jess in her sky; B's recorded run added at the
owner's choice); seed:22f46bc3c31add97
