An AI agent for your household that asks before it acts.

Jessbot runs on your own computer. Everyone at home gets a private chat with Jess, who looks things up in her own browser, keeps working when the app is closed, and stops for your OK before she sends, spends or books anything.

Then open http://127.0.0.1:3737. Node.js and the other systems are in the install guide. Free and open source, early preview.

One request, start to finish

This is the run that card came from, recorded on a test install and sped up where it waits.

  1. You ask in plain words. “In the background, find one easy hike near Lyon for Saturday morning, then email Maya the plan.”
  2. Jess takes it to the background. She opens her own side chat for it, so yours stays free, and searches the web with her browser.
  3. She stops at a card. The recipient, the exact text, whether it can be undone, and why she is asking. Nothing has left yet.
  4. You approve, and only then it is sent. The activity log keeps a line for each step.

What else it does

One assistant for the whole household, with a private space for each person.

A feed written for you

Say what you want to follow in plain words. Jess picks sources (news searches, RSS, your own OPML) and writes short posts, starting with a brief for your morning or evening. “Less of this” and likes shape the next pick.

Safety and privacy, decided in code

The assistant is useful because it can act, so what it may do is enforced around the model, not only written in its instructions.

What it won't do on its own

  • Send, spend, book or share without a card. Mail, calendar changes, purchases, bookings, shared memories and uploads stop for your OK with the exact text. After it has read a web page or an email, even “always allow” asks again.
  • Take orders from a web page. Pages, emails, feeds and files reach the model fenced as information, with hidden text removed and text aimed at AI assistants flagged.
  • Run commands on your machine. There is no shell tool. Pages it builds run in a sandbox with no network.
  • Reach your home network. Its browser can't open your router, NAS or admin panels unless the admin allows it.
  • Spend past its budget. A weekly budget per person and a cap per task.

What leaves your machine

  • What you and the assistant send to your model provider: the conversation, and the pages or files it is working on.
  • Web traffic from its browser when it looks things up, and feed checks.
  • Your mail and calendar servers, if you connect them, and push notifications through your browser's push service.
  • Nothing else: no telemetry, no account with us. Chats, memories and files stay in its data/ folder.

Prompt injection is mitigated, not solved. The security model says what is defended, and what isn't.

Run it at home

You need Docker or Node.js 24.19 or newer, about 1 GB of disk, and a key for an AI model (OpenRouter by default, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint).

git clone https://github.com/anefzaoui/jessbot
cd jessbot
docker compose up -d --build
docker compose logs jessbot | grep "setup code"
git clone https://github.com/anefzaoui/jessbot
cd jessbot
npm ci
npm run build
npm start

Open http://127.0.0.1:3737, enter the setup code from the log, and the setup page walks you through the rest: your account, the model, the browser, the assistant's name and look, and the people you invite.

Early preview. It works, but it is young. Run it on your own computer or home network, or behind a VPN, not on the open internet yet.
