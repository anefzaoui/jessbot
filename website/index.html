Jess keeps working after you close the app.

Jessbot is a self-hosted AI agent for your household. Jess looks things up in her own browser, writes, reminds and watches for you, day and night, and stops to ask before she sends, spends or books anything.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Early preview: it runs on your own computer, then open 127.0.0.1:3737. Other ways to install

08:00 I keep reminding you until it's done.

Medicine, the bins, homework. Gentler, then firmer nudges until someone says done, never in quiet hours. Quiet watches too: “tell me when the fare drops” is checked on a schedule and only speaks when something changed.

Sending an email, changing a calendar, buying, booking, sharing a memory, uploading a file. Each one stops at a card with the exact text, who it reaches, whether it can be undone and why I'm asking. It goes out only when you say so.

House lights up. Here's the rest of the show.

One assistant for everyone at home. Each person gets a private chat; the admin decides what Jess may do for whom, and how much she may spend a week.

She calls standby. You call go.

What Jess may do is decided in code, not only in her instructions.

Waits for your go

  • Sending an email
  • Changing a calendar
  • Buying or booking
  • Sharing a memory
  • Uploading a file to a website

After she has read a web page or an email, “always allow” no longer covers anything that sends or spends.

Never on her board

  • No shell and no code running on your machine
  • No reaching your local network, unless the admin allows it
  • No pasting your keys or passwords into a form or an email
  • No instructions taken from pages, feeds or emails

Spending limits per task and per person each week. Prompt injection is mitigated, not solved: the security model lists the known limits.

What leaves the building.

Chats, memories, files and settings stay in the data/ folder on your machine. No telemetry, no account with us. Only three things go out:

  1. Your model provider the conversation and the pages or files she's working on. OpenRouter, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible service.
  2. Her browser's web traffic the searches and sites she visits, weather and maps, and feed polling.
  3. Your mail, calendar and push only if you connect them.

Bring Jess home.

Node.js 24.19 or newer, or Docker. Windows and Linux; macOS should work. The setup page asks for the code printed in the server's log, then your model key, her browser, her name and look, and the people at home. Getting started
