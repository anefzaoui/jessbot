# Jessbot guide

This guide is for the people who set up and use Jessbot at home: the admin who installs it and everyone who talks to
it. It explains what each part does and how to use it, in plain words. If you're new, read the pages in this order;
later, jump to whichever one you need.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install Jessbot, finish the setup wizard and invite your household.
2. [Name and look](name-and-look.md): give the assistant its own name, face, pronouns and manner.
3. [Models and costs](providers.md): choose the AI model, what it costs, and how to keep spending in check.
4. [People and admin](people-and-admin.md): invite people, keep chats private, decide what the assistant may do for
   each person, and sign in safely.
5. [Approvals and safety](approvals-and-safety.md): what stops for your OK, what an approval card shows, and the limits
   that hold whatever the model says.
6. [The Library](library.md): everything the assistant makes and every file you give it, with versions, previews and
   export.
7. [Your Feed](feed.md): tell it what to follow and get short posts written for you.
8. [Mail and calendar](mail-and-calendar.md): connect your mailbox and calendar, and what the assistant may do with
   them.
9. [Backups and updates](backups-and-updates.md): daily backups, restoring, updating and moving to another machine.
10. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): common problems and how to fix them.
11. [Frequently asked questions](faq.md): cost, privacy, phones, languages and more.
